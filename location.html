Location

Mashhad-e-Hind is in the town of Samana, Patiala district, Punjab. Samana lies on State Highway 10 between Patiala and Patran.

Open in Google Maps for directions from where you are.

Getting here

  • By road: from Patiala, about 27 km south-west on State Highway 10.
  • By rail: the nearest major railway station is Patiala.
  • By air: the nearest airport is Chandigarh, about 95 km away.
  • Within Samana: the shrine is widely known as Panj Peer Mazar, so asking for it by that name works well. For help on the way, please call a member of the committee.
