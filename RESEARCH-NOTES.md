# Research notes: Mashhad-e-Hind and Samana

Compiled 20 October 2026 for the website's History page. These notes list what each
statement on the site rests on, and what the committee needs to confirm.

**Limits of this research.** Searches were run from a restricted environment that could
read search-result summaries but could not open most source pages in full. Every claim
below should be checked against the original source (and, for the inscription, against
the stone itself) before the committee approves the wording.

## Sources consulted

1. Wikipedia, "Samana, India" / "Samana, Punjab" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samana,_India
   Town history: Raja Jaipal; Qutb ud-Din Aibak 1192; Ain-i-Akbari pargana of Sirhind
   sarkar; centre of Shia scholars until 1947; Panj Peer Mazar inscription.
2. mashadehind.in, "About Samana" — https://mashadehind.in/about-samana/
   Appears to be an existing site connected with the shrine. Location on State
   Highway 10 between Patiala and Patran; inscription of 967 AH / 1559 by Ajruddin Khan
   Mughal; verification by scholars and Iranian embassy officials.
   **Ask the committee whether this is their site.** If so, the new site should reuse its
   approved wording and the two should not contradict each other.
3. V. S. Gill (?), "Imami Shrine at Samana: Most Noteworthy and Neglected", Heritage Guru
   blog, Nov 2014 — http://vsgoi.blogspot.com/2014/11/style-definitions-table.html
   Inscription naming Imam Mashhad Ali, son of Imam Ali Musa Raza; Ramadan 967 AH, year 4
   of Akbar; found during cleaning before a visit by Justice Shabbar Rizvi of the Lahore
   High Court; notes that some sources name Imam Reza's third son as Yahya, not Mashhad Ali.
   (Author name inferred from the blog address; confirm.)
4. Battle of Samana (Wikipedia), learnpunjabi.org Encyclopaedia of Sikhism (Banda Singh
   Bahadur), Britannica (Sikhism, 18th century) — Banda Singh Bahadur took Samana on
   about 26 November 1709.
5. YouTube: "Salana Majlis Mashhad-e-Hind, Samana Punjab, Maulana Kalbe Rushaid Rizvi
   2025" — evidence that an annual majlis is held; date not stated.
6. Facebook pages: "Roza-e-Imam Mashhad Ali A.S, Samana, Punjab"; "Dargah hazrat mashhad
   ali samana punjab"; "E-imam Mashhad Ali Samana" (facebook.com/pajpeer.samana). One lists
   a phone number and email; **not copied to the site** until the committee confirms which
   contact details are official.

## Left off the site on purpose

These appear in sources on Samana's history but were left out because they are
sensitive. The committee may wish to decide how, or whether, to mention them:

- Several sources say Samana was home to the executioners of Guru Tegh Bahadur (1675) and
  of the younger Sahibzadas, and that this was one reason Banda Singh Bahadur attacked the
  town in November 1709. Accounts of the sack vary widely.
- A dispute over land near the shrine after 1947, resolved when the government allotted
  land about 200 m away for a gurdwara (reported in search summaries; not verified).
